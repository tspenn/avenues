## Fuel Conservation

For a long time the country treated a missing plant as a law of nature. It was never nature. It was a pile of constraints that looked like physics because nobody with a reason to look had taken them apart.

Some of those constraints have moved. Not in a speech. In the actual work of building heavy infrastructure — what it costs, how long it takes, and which parts of the old “impossible” were only a habit. What used to close the argument no longer closes it.

That is all this page will say.

A short paper sits behind the gate. It is written for people who have to decide whether to build, and for people who presently run the plants. If that is you, write info@skylandreach.com and ask for the password. It is not posted. It is not given without knowing who is asking.
