# Swamp Building

*At the water's edge.*

*Skyland Reach LLC*

Coming soon.

## The Problem

A great deal of American land sits where the ground is wet, the rules are hostile, and a conventional house is either forbidden or a slow insurance claim. The edge of a wetland is treated as a problem. It is also a place people already want to live.

Zoning, flood maps, and foundation practice were written for dry lots. They do not describe this ground honestly, and they do not give a builder a clean yes.

## The Approach

We are working on how to build there: the structure, and the zoning argument that has to travel with it. The page will stay thin until that work is ready to describe. What will not be published here is a set of drawings someone can take and run with.
